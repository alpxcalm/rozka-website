      Questions

      What owners ask us first.

      Do my workers need a smartphone or a login?

      No. Workers only punch at the device at your door, with their face, finger, card or QR. Only you and your managers log in, from any browser.

      What happens when the internet goes down?

      Attendance keeps working. It is saved on the machine and sent with the right time once the internet is back.

      Does Rozka send salaries from my bank account?

      No. You pay the way you do today. When you mark a month as paid, Rozka saves who got how much and when, so nobody can change it later, and makes a salary slip for each person.

      What about PF, ESI and TDS?

      You type them in as cuts and they show on the salary slip. Rozka doesn't calculate them for you.

      Prices start at ₹119 per employee per month, inclusive of all taxes, and may vary based on your business requirements.
